# Where each task comes from (checked 13 Oct 2026)

Each of the five tasks is built on an activity or idea in the current NCERT textbooks for Grades 6–8 (all marked "Reprint 2026-27").
Each row below was checked against the PDF downloaded from ncert.nic.in on 13 Oct 2026. Page numbers are pages within the chapter PDF.

| Task | Book and chapter | What the book says | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G6 Science: river on the map | Curiosity (Grade 6), Ch. 5 Measurement of Length and Motion, §5.4 | "In the case of a curved line, measurements can be made with the help of a flexible measuring tape or by using a thread" | [fecu105.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/fecu105.pdf) | 9 (printed 87) |
| G6 Maths: same fence | Ganita Prakash (Grade 6), Ch. 6 Perimeter and Area | Chapter summary: "Two closed figures can have the same area with different perimeters, or the same perimeter with different areas." | [fegp106.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/fegp106.pdf) | 22 (printed 150) |
| G7 Science: pendulum | Curiosity (Grade 7), Ch. 8 Measurement of Time and Motion | Activity 8.2: time 10 oscillations of a 100 cm pendulum; "Repeat Activity 8.2 … with pendulums of two or three different lengths. Does the time period change?" | [gecu108.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/gecu108.pdf) | 5–6 |
| G8 Science: salt | Curiosity (Grade 8), Ch. 9 The Amazing World of Solutes, Solvents, and Solutions | Activity 9.1: add spoons of salt to half a glass of water; "How many spoons of salt were you able to dissolve before some of it remained undissolved?" Activity 9.2 (temperature) | [hecu109.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hecu109.pdf) | 3–4 |
| G8 Maths: same sweetness | Ganita Prakash (Grade 8), Ch. 7 Proportional Reasoning-1 | Example 2: lemonade with 10 spoons of sugar in 6 glasses; "a : b :: c : d indicates that the ratios a : b and c : d are proportional." | [hegp107.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hegp107.pdf) | 4 |

Chapter lists for all six books were checked from the prelims PDFs (fegp1ps, fecu1ps, gegp1ps, gecu1ps, hegp1ps, hecu1ps).

The virtual labs use the standard relationships (pendulum period, solubility rising with temperature) with a hidden constant per student. Their tolerances are kept private so they cannot be used to fake answers.
