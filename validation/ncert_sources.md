# Where each task comes from (checked 13 Oct 2026)

Each of the five tasks is built on an activity or idea in the current NCERT textbooks for Grades 6–8 (all marked "Reprint 2026-27").
Each row below was checked against the PDF downloaded from ncert.nic.in on 13 Oct 2026. Page numbers are pages within the chapter PDF.

| Task | Book and chapter | What the book says | Source | Page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G6 Science: handspans | Curiosity (Grade 6), Ch. 5 Measurement of Length and Motion | Students measure a table in handspans and find "the number of handspans is different" for each person; Activity 5.1 "Let us measure" | [fecu105.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/fecu105.pdf) | 2–3, 8 |
| G7 Science: pendulum | Curiosity (Grade 7), Ch. 8 Measurement of Time and Motion | Activity 8.2: time 10 oscillations of a 100 cm pendulum; "Repeat Activity 8.2 … with pendulums of two or three different lengths. Does the time period change?" | [gecu108.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/gecu108.pdf) | 5–6 |
| G7 Maths: paper thickness | Ganita Prakash (Grade 7), Ch. 3 A Peek Beyond the Point | Decimals for measurement, down to thousandths ("How many thousandths make one unit?") | [gegp103.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/gegp103.pdf) | 16 |
| G8 Science: fix the AI (salt) | Curiosity (Grade 8), Ch. 9 The Amazing World of Solutes, Solvents, and Solutions | Activity 9.1: add spoons of salt to half a glass of water; "How many spoons of salt were you able to dissolve before some of it remained undissolved?" Activity 9.2 (temperature) is a demonstration activity | [hecu109.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hecu109.pdf) | 3–4 |
| G8 Maths: same sweetness | Ganita Prakash (Grade 8), Ch. 7 Proportional Reasoning-1 | Example 2: "Kesang wanted to make lemonade … 6 glasses … 10 spoons of sugar"; "a : b :: c : d indicates that the ratios a : b and c : d are proportional." | [hegp107.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/hegp107.pdf) | 4 |

Chapter lists for all six books were checked from the prelims PDFs (fegp1ps, fecu1ps, gegp1ps, gecu1ps, hegp1ps, hecu1ps).

The server-side checks use standard physics and geometry for each activity. Their tolerances are kept private so they cannot be used to fake answers.
