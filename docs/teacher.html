For teachers

How to use: pick a task, send its link (or print the sheet), and the next day spend 2 minutes on the class check. No login, no setup, nothing to mark online.

What students bring back: their answer sheet (in the notebook, printed, or as a photo) with the sentence a family member said back to them.

How to check in 2 minutes: ask the class-check questions on a fresh case, with phones away, and ask two students to read their family member’s sentence. Use the rubric to sort a few notebooks into Got it, Partly or Not yet.

About AI: an AI can write a convincing answer sheet for every task. It cannot measure the student’s table or taste their drink. The class check shows who learned it.
