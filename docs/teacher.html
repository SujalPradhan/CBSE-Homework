For teachers

How to use: send the link for a task. Students do it at home and get a result card with a code. You only look at the card. There is nothing to mark.

What the card is: a signed result (Got it / Partly / Not yet) with a code. Open the card page and paste a code to check that it is genuine. A student cannot change the level, because the code is signed.

What “verified” means: (1) the student’s measurements agree with each other, and (2) their written answers met this task’s rubric, as judged by an AI checker. It does not prove the activity was done: an AI can write convincing answers and invent numbers. Use the card as a first filter.

If you want certainty: two optional things take about 2 minutes. Ask the class-check question (below) with phones away, and ask two students to say what question their family member asked them. Identical codes from different students on the same day mean identical answers.

Privacy: students’ answers are sent to the checker and are not stored. The card shows no name.
